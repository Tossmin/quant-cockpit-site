Quant Cockpit Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 11, 2026

Quant Cockpit is committed to minimizing the personal information required to operate the application and protecting authentication information used for third-party integrations.

1. Scope of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when users access Quant Cockpit, activate a software license, use application functionality, or connect a supported third-party service such as Alpaca.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features used and the final production configuration, Quant Cockpit may process the following categories of information:

  • Quant Cockpit license identifier.
  • Application account or user identifier, if account functionality is enabled.
  • Technical information required for license validation and security.
  • Application version and basic connection-status information.
  • Alpaca OAuth authorization information.
  • Alpaca OAuth access token when required to maintain an authorized connection.
  • Market data associated with the authenticated user's authorized connection.
  • Calculated analytics generated from authorized market data.
  • Technical logs necessary for security, troubleshooting, and service operation.

3. Information We Do Not Request

Quant Cockpit does not ask users to provide their Alpaca account password directly to Quant Cockpit.

Alpaca account authentication is performed through Alpaca's own authorization process.

Quant Cockpit does not intentionally request payment-card information directly when payment is processed by an external marketplace or payment provider.

4. Purposes of Processing

Information may be processed only as reasonably necessary for purposes such as:

  • Providing Quant Cockpit functionality.
  • Authenticating and validating software licenses.
  • Connecting a user's Alpaca account through OAuth.
  • Retrieving market data authorized for that user.
  • Calculating and displaying market analytics.
  • Displaying indicator-based directional estimates.
  • Maintaining application and account security.
  • Detecting unauthorized or abusive use.
  • Troubleshooting technical problems.
  • Responding to user support requests.

5. Alpaca OAuth

When a user chooses to connect an Alpaca account, the user is redirected to Alpaca to authenticate and authorize the connection.

Quant Cockpit does not receive the user's Alpaca account password.

After authorization, Quant Cockpit may receive an OAuth authorization code and an access token required to access permissions authorized by the user.

Quant Cockpit is intended to request only permissions necessary for its read-only and market-data functionality.

Quant Cockpit is not intended to request trading permission for order execution and does not place, modify, cancel, or automatically execute securities orders.

6. User Data Separation

Authentication credentials and data obtained under one user's authorized connection are intended to be logically separated from information belonging to other users.

Market data obtained through User A's Alpaca authorization will not be intentionally redistributed or displayed to User B through Quant Cockpit.

Each authenticated user's authorized market data is intended to be used only to provide functionality and analytics to that same authenticated user.

7. OAuth Token and Credential Security

OAuth access tokens and other authentication credentials are treated as sensitive information.

OAuth Client Secrets will not be intentionally embedded in publicly distributed application files, public GitHub repositories, or public website source code.

Authentication information used by production versions of Quant Cockpit will be protected using appropriate server-side security controls.

8. Market Data and Calculated Analytics

Market data accessed through an authorized Alpaca connection is intended to be used for displaying market information and calculating analytics for the authenticated user.

Quant Cockpit may calculate numerical indicators and directional estimates from authorized market data.

Quant Cockpit does not intend to use one customer's authorized raw market-data entitlement to supply another customer with that customer's market data.

9. Data Retention

Personal information, authentication information, and operational information will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the applicable Quant Cockpit service, maintain security, comply with applicable legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

OAuth credentials may be deleted, invalidated, or cease to be used when a user disconnects the associated account, authorization is revoked, or the applicable Quant Cockpit service relationship ends, subject to technical and legal retention requirements.

Technical logs may be retained for a limited period for security, operational, fraud-prevention, and troubleshooting purposes.

10. Third-Party Services

Quant Cockpit may rely on third-party services to provide certain functionality.

These services may include:

  • Alpaca for OAuth authorization and market-data access.
  • Cloud infrastructure providers for backend services and security.
  • GitHub Pages for the public Quant Cockpit informational website.
  • Online marketplaces or payment providers used by customers to purchase the application.

Third-party services operate under their own terms, privacy policies, technical requirements, and data-processing practices.

11. Sharing and Disclosure

Quant Cockpit does not intend to sell users' personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

Information may be processed by service providers only to the extent reasonably necessary to operate, secure, support, or provide the application, or when disclosure is required by applicable law.

12. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards will be used to protect authentication information and other data processed by Quant Cockpit.

These safeguards may include access controls, encrypted communications, credential separation, and server-side protection of sensitive secrets.

However, no internet-connected system or software service can guarantee absolute security.

13. User Choices and Account Disconnection

Users may choose not to connect an Alpaca account.

Users may revoke or disconnect an authorized Alpaca connection through available Alpaca or Quant Cockpit controls when such functionality is available.

Requests concerning personal information or Quant Cockpit account data may be submitted using the contact information below.

14. License and Security Information

Quant Cockpit may process a license identifier and limited technical information for software activation, license validation, fraud prevention, abuse detection, and unauthorized redistribution prevention.

Such information will be used only for legitimate software operation, security, licensing, and support purposes.

15. Children's Privacy

Quant Cockpit is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of providing securities-market analytics.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Quant Cockpit features, third-party integrations, applicable legal requirements, or data-processing practices change.

The effective date shown at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
