Quant Cockpit Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 11, 2026

Quant Cockpit is committed to minimizing the personal information required to operate the application and protecting authentication information used for third-party integrations.

1. Scope of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when users access Quant Cockpit, activate a software license, or connect a supported third-party service such as Alpaca.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features used, Quant Cockpit may process the following categories of information:

  • Quant Cockpit license identifier.
  • Application account or user identifier, if account functionality is enabled.
  • Technical information required for license validation and security.
  • Application version and basic connection-status information.
  • Alpaca OAuth authorization information.
  • Alpaca OAuth access token, when required to maintain an authorized connection.
  • Market data associated with the authenticated user's authorized connection.
  • Technical logs necessary for security, troubleshooting, and service operation.

3. Information We Do Not Request

Quant Cockpit does not ask users to provide their Alpaca account password directly to Quant Cockpit.

Alpaca account authentication is performed through Alpaca's own authorization process.

Quant Cockpit does not intentionally request payment-card information directly when payment is processed by an external marketplace or payment platform.

4. Purposes of Processing

Information is processed only as necessary for purposes such as:

  • Providing Quant Cockpit functionality.
  • Authenticating valid software licenses.
  • Connecting a user's Alpaca account through OAuth.
  • Retrieving market data authorized for that user.
  • Calculating and displaying market analytics.
  • Maintaining application security.
  • Detecting unauthorized or abusive use.
  • Troubleshooting technical problems.
  • Responding to user support requests.

5. Alpaca OAuth

When a user chooses to connect an Alpaca account, the user is redirected to Alpaca to authenticate and authorize the connection.

Quant Cockpit does not receive the user's Alpaca password.

After authorization, Quant Cockpit may receive an OAuth authorization code and access token required to access the permissions granted by the user.

Quant Cockpit is intended to request only permissions necessary for read-only market-data functionality and does not request trading permission for order execution.

6. User Data Separation

Data obtained under one user's authorized connection is intended to be logically separated from data belonging to other users.

Market data obtained through User A's authorization is not intended to be redistributed or displayed to User B through Quant Cockpit.

7. OAuth Token Security

OAuth access tokens and other authentication credentials are treated as sensitive information.

OAuth Client Secrets are not intended to be embedded in publicly distributed application files or public website source code.

Authentication information used by production versions of Quant Cockpit will be protected using appropriate server-side security controls.

8. Data Retention

Personal information and authentication information will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant Quant Cockpit service, maintain security, comply with applicable legal requirements, or resolve disputes.

OAuth credentials may be deleted or invalidated when a user disconnects the associated account, authorization is revoked, or the Quant Cockpit account or service relationship ends, subject to technical and legal retention requirements.

Technical logs may be retained for a limited period for security, operational, and troubleshooting purposes.

9. Third-Party Services

Quant Cockpit may rely on third-party services to provide certain functionality.

These may include:

  • Alpaca for OAuth authorization and authorized market-data access.
  • Cloud infrastructure providers for application backend services.
  • GitHub Pages for the public Quant Cockpit information website.
  • Online marketplaces or payment providers used by customers to purchase the application.

Third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

10. Market Data

Market data accessed through an authorized Alpaca connection is used for the purpose of displaying market information and calculating analytics for the authenticated user.

Quant Cockpit does not intend to use one customer's authorized raw market data to provide another customer with that customer's market-data entitlement.

11. Sale of Personal Information

Quant Cockpit does not sell users' personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

12. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards will be used to protect authentication information and other data processed by Quant Cockpit.

However, no internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security.

13. User Choices

Users may choose not to connect an Alpaca account.

Users may revoke an authorized Alpaca connection through available account or authorization controls.

Requests concerning personal information or Quant Cockpit account data may be submitted through the contact information below.

14. Children's Privacy

Quant Cockpit is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of providing investment-market analytics.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Quant Cockpit features, third-party integrations, legal requirements, or data-processing practices change.

Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date shown at the top of this page.
