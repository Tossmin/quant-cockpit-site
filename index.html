Quant Cockpit

U.S. Stock Market Data Analytics & Visualization

About Quant Cockpit

Quant Cockpit is a read-only market data analytics and visualization application for U.S. equities.

The application organizes market data and calculated indicators into an easy-to-read dashboard so users can review market information efficiently.

Information & Analytics

Quant Cockpit displays market information, calculated indicators, numerical analytics, and indicator-based directional estimates.

LONG/SHORT directional estimates are calculated from market data and indicators. They are estimates only and are not recommendations to buy, sell, enter, exit, or hold any security or position.

Read-Only Application

Quant Cockpit does not place, modify, cancel, or automatically execute orders.

The application does not provide personalized investment advice. All investment and trading decisions are made independently by the user.
